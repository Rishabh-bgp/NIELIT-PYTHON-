# Code of conduct

This repository is a public teaching project. Discussion in issues, pull requests, and reviews is part of the material: a learner may read it later. Participants are expected to keep that discussion accurate and usable.

## Expected behaviour

- Comment on the code, the explanation, and the change. Do not comment on the person.
- Assume a question is asked in good faith unless the message shows otherwise.
- Give the file, the cell, and the result when you report a failure.
- Accept a correction of a technical claim when the notebook or the language reference disagrees with it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, harassment, or personal remarks about a contributor.
- Publishing private information about another person.
- Submitting work that presents someone else's notebook as your own without attribution. The MIT Licence allows reuse; it does not make misrepresentation acceptable in a review.

## Maintainer response

The maintainer may edit, close, or lock a thread that does not meet this standard, and may reject a pull request that does. A disagreement about design is not, by itself, a breach.

Questions about this document can be raised as an issue on the repository.
